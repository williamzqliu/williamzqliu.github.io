This was the final project for Visual Cognition: a mind map summarizing the course. I organized course material into diagrams of visual processing, object recognition, and visual search.

I used selected lines from Katy Perry's *Witness* as headings, connecting the lyrics to course topics through creative associations. Connector lines in the full poster link those lyrics to the corresponding diagrams. The panels use numbered sequences, branching pathways, and a search loop to show different relationships.

I drew the diagrams, choosing some structures myself and redrawing or adapting others from references. The eye and brain illustration is a stock vector credited below. The poster borrows its palette and layout from Spotify Wrapped.
